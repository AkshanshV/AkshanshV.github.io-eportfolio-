Goals & Objectives

My primary goal right now is to transition theoretical IT knowledge into practical expertise by mastering core enterprise platforms like Microsoft Intune, Entra, and Tenable. Understanding how to manage device configurations and applications at an enterprise scale is essential to bridging the gap between security and continuous business operations.

I am also highly focused on driving proactive problem-solving through automation. Rather than manually fixing vulnerabilities one by one, I aim to apply the Systems Development Life Cycle (SDLC) in a live environment to script and automate repetitive security patches and endpoint configurations across the network.

Finally, a major objective of mine is to sharpen my technical communication skills. Navigating a massive utility company requires bridging the gap between IT and the rest of the organization—translating complex technical fixes into plain, accessible language for end-users, while simultaneously ensuring my backend documentation remains highly detailed and precise for my technical colleagues.
